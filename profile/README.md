## The lab — ten years further out

Pazity climbs the first two rungs today — *trusted execution*, then *execution that compounds*.
The lab works on the third: **goals that evolve themselves** — systems that ask what is most worth doing, with proof required for every promotion.

Open questions we work on:

- **Goal critics & anti-drift** — systems that rewrite objectives must prove they haven't drifted.
- **Value density & alignment** — scoring what matters, not what is merely measurable.
- **Forgetting & detox** — forget cleanly, and prove the poison is gone.
- **Provenance-preserving substrate** — every inherited skill carries where it came from and why it was trusted.

Safety thesis: **answerable superintelligence** — replayable audit on every action, permission evaluated before intelligence, and a human veto at the top that never expires.
